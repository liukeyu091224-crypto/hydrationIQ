Roadmap

How the idea could evolve.

Judges usually want to see not only the idea, but how you plan to test and improve it. A clean roadmap makes your website look more serious and complete.

01

User research

Interview student athletes and active users to understand when hydration reminders actually feel useful.

02

Interactive prototype

Build a testable concept with sport selection, environment inputs, and scheduling logic.

03

Pilot testing

Compare training-aware reminders with fixed reminders and collect perceived usefulness and interruption data.

04

Iteration

Refine the experience, clarify claims, and only strengthen the science after appropriate validation.
