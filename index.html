Our mission

Make hydration guidance easier to use.

Hydration IQ is a student-led concept developed for an innovation challenge. We want to explore whether guidance that fits people's actual training schedules can be more useful than generic reminders.

We are developing and evaluating this idea. Product features and outcomes described on this site are proposed unless explicitly identified as tested.
